# DadoFit V12.2 - Identity, Workspaces & RBAC

## Goal
One authentication identity, multiple operating contexts. A personal Gymbro can create/manage Squads. Gym and Brand powers come only from organization workspaces and membership roles.

## Integrated validation
1. Existing Nelson Bro users still log in and get a Personal workspace.
2. Existing Gym owners also see their Gym workspace in the selector.
3. Personal workspace:
   - /app works as normal.
   - Gymbros, 1v1 and Squads remain available.
   - Gym institutional challenge buttons are not shown.
   - Organization invitations can still be accepted from /organizations.
4. Gym workspace:
   - Dashboard appears at /workspace.
   - /app acts as an institutional dice generator, not personal completion.
   - Owner/Admin/Coach can publish Gym challenge or Gym vs Gym from the selected Gym only.
   - Member cannot publish institutional challenges.
5. Brand workspace:
   - Brand dashboard appears.
   - Personal/Gym routes are not available in this context.
   - Brand campaigns remain reserved for V13.
6. Registration:
   - Personal creates only a personal account.
   - Gym registration enters Gym onboarding.
   - Brand registration enters Brand onboarding.
7. Server guard:
   - Personal accounts cannot call create_organization to create a Gym or Brand.
   - Organization challenges can only be created by organization_type=gym.
