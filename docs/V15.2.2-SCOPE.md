# DadoFit V15.2.2

## Scope

- Fixes missing notifications when a verified Gym publishes a reward.
- Whenever a Gym transitions a reward to `active`, active members who have never been notified about that reward receive an announcement.
- Notification includes Gym name, reward title, DC cost and `/rewards` route metadata.
- The same member/reward combination is never notified twice, so pause/reactivate cannot create notification spam.
- This also lets already-published test rewards be validated after installing the hotfix: pause and activate the reward once; members who never received the announcement will get it.
- Brand rewards are intentionally not broadcast globally yet; audience segmentation belongs to a later commercial milestone.
- No changes to redemption, wallet deductions, QR fulfillment, coupon pools or anti-farming.
